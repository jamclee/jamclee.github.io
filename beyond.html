A Cappella & Arranging

I have been singing a cappella since high school. At Michigan I sing with the GradTONES, the university’s graduate student a cappella group (the name stands for “Troupe Of Needlessly Educated Singers”), and I arrange music for the group. A few of the arrangements I am proudest of:

Community & Background

Ph.D. Community

I serve on the Ross Ph.D. Forum, as Well-Being Chair (2024–2026) and previously Facilities Co-chair (2023–2024), and volunteered answering prospective and new students’ questions about the program.

Before Academia

I spent two years as a data engineer at J.P. Morgan Chase, where I also helped recruit underrepresented and non-traditional talent into tech. I later did user research at Levi Strauss & Co. on its omnichannel shopping platforms.
