A Cappella & Arranging

I have been singing a cappella since high school. At Michigan I sing with the GradTONES, the university’s graduate student a cappella group (the name stands for “Troupe Of Needlessly Educated Singers”), and I arrange music for the group. A few of the arrangements I am proudest of:
